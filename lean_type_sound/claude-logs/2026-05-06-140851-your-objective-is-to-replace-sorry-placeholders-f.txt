  Directives:                                                                   
                                                                                
  Step 1: Structural Scaffolding: Do not attempt a monolithic proof for complex 
   theorems. Scaffold the proof first using have statements to establish        
  intermediate truths and bridge logical gaps.                                  
                                                                                
  Step 2: Attribute Annotations: When extracting intermediate steps into        
  standalone helper lemmas, tag them with appropriate Lean 4 attributes (such   
  as @[simp], @[aesop safe/unsafe], @[ext], or @[refl]) to power up subsequent  
  automation. Choose the most fitting attribute for the lemma's structure.      
                                                                                
  Step 3: Heavy-Hitting Tactics: Leverage Mathlib 4's highest-level automation. 
   Always try aesop for search-based resolution, omega for Presburger           
  arithmetic, linarith for linear inequalities, and simp_all for context        
  simplification.                                                               
                                                                                
  Step 4: Depth and Refinement: If a goal remains open, inspect the remaining   
  type signature deeply. Unfold definitions dynamically and search for the      
  exact Mathlib 4 lemmas required. If a tactic fails, do not blindly repeat it. 
   Analyze the resulting error state, adjust your have scaffolding, and apply a 
   more specific tactic sequence.
